Privacy Policy

Burp Rating - Funny Sound App

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Burp Rating ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

Burp Rating does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All audio processing happens locally on your device.

Audio Recordings

When you use the App to record audio, the recording is processed entirely on your device. Audio data is:

  • Processed locally for analysis
  • Stored only on your device
  • Never transmitted to external servers
  • Deleted when you remove the entry or uninstall the App

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is designed for entertainment purposes only.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
