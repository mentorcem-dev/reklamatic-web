Recommended working model

A campaign people can join.

Eluniboss wants music to circulate through different people’s accounts. A creator campaign is the primary model: participants make their own approved edits or original videos, publish on their own eligible accounts, then submit the post link and analytics. Dedicated accounts are an optional second layer for a continuous artist presence.

  1. Agree the brief.Choose the song, official sound, formats, Spain audience requirement, engagement threshold, rate and total reward budget.
  2. Invite suitable creators.Recruit Spanish-speaking creators and accounts with evidenced audiences in Spain. Participation depends on creator interest and the final brief.
  3. Create, publish and submit.Creators use approved assets and required artist credits. Each submission includes its public link and post-specific analytics.
  4. Validate and pay.Check the creative rules, authentic engagement, eligible geography and counting window. Only qualifying posts earn, within the agreed caps and remaining budget.

We manage briefing, creator coordination, submission review and reporting. The statement of work identifies the payout platform, any management/platform charges and the net creator reward pool before funding.

Engagement is an eligibility condition

Views alone do not unlock payment.

Proposed minimum · confirm in the final brief
2% like rate

Like rate = likes ÷ video views × 100. At 10,000 views, a post needs at least 200 genuine likes. A post below the agreed threshold is ineligible for payment at the final validation snapshot.

This is a clear, measurable engagement gate—not a universal industry rate. Comments, shares and saves are also reported separately where platform analytics provide them.

The evidence must match the post

  • At least 1,000 eligible views within the agreed window.
  • The agreed engagement rate and Spain audience requirement.
  • Public post link, correct official audio and required credits.
  • Post-specific analytics, including geography where available; no substitution of account-wide averages.
  • No purchased views, likes, engagement pods or manipulated screenshots.

Eluniboss gets one clear threshold, formula and assessment date, written into the brief before creators join. For example, 10,000 views and 150 likes would fail a 2% gate; 10,000 views and 250 likes would pass that gate, subject to all other requirements. Approved entitlements follow the disclosed rules; criteria are not changed retrospectively.

The audience you want

Creator campaign

Recruit creators whose analytics show a relevant audience in Spain. Use Spanish-language hooks, local cultural references and Spain-friendly posting times. Validate the agreed minimum Spain share with post-level evidence.

For a strictly Spain-only reward, only verified Spanish views qualify. If the platform cannot provide reliable post-level country evidence, that post cannot be sold as verified Spain-only delivery.

Dedicated accounts

We can configure a Spain-focused account operation, language, profile context and publishing schedule. This provides more control over positioning and output, while audience geography is still checked in analytics.

A VPN, proxy or account location does not prove that viewers are in Spain and is not a substitute for audience verification.

The $0.50 example is a proposed pilot rate, not a guaranteed price for Spanish viewers. Final geography rules may change creator availability, reward rate and delivery time. Views measure plays, not unique people, Spotify streams or sales.

Working model 2 · Dedicated distribution

An ongoing publishing network
built around your artist.

A dedicated account is a social profile devoted to one artist or project. Instead of recruiting people for one release, we set up and operate an agreed group of artist-focused pages that publish every day. You provide the direction and approve the content; Reklamatic handles preparation, editing, scheduling and reporting.

This model suits a catalogue that should keep circulating between releases: music videos, live performances, interviews, podcast appearances, archive moments and new announcements. Accounts follow the agreed editorial identity and credit the artist clearly; they are not presented as unrelated people independently endorsing the artist.

Concrete example: 30 platform accounts could mean 15 Instagram accounts and 15 TikTok accounts. At 5 posts per account per day, that is 150 daily publications and 4,500 publications over 30 days, for $5,400 + tax per month. An Instagram profile and its TikTok counterpart count as two accounts.
$180 + tax / account / month

One dedicated price across approved content formats. Editing, captions, publishing and account reporting are scoped together. Content can come from your approved artist assets or an agreed production plan.

Publishing basis: 5 posts per account per day across a 30-day publishing month: 150 posts per account. Each platform account counts separately. Publications are not necessarily unique productions; the asset and originality scope is written into the order.

What we do each month

  1. Set up the account network.Agree the artist, account count and platform mix. Prepare names, profile images, bios, artist tags and destination links. Configure the language, editorial context and publishing schedule for Spain.
  2. Organise the source library.You share approved music videos, interviews, podcasts, performances and archive files. We organise usable moments by song, theme and format so the network has a coherent content plan.
  3. Prepare the short videos.Choose strong moments, reframe for vertical viewing, add subtitles, write opening hooks and prepare platform-appropriate captions. Ready-made clips can also enter the publishing plan.
  4. Collect your approvals.Review the initial creative direction and scheduled batches through the agreed approval workflow. Revision notes guide later edits. Unapproved material stays outside the publishing queue.
  5. Publish consistently.Schedule 5 posts per active account per day after preparation. Rotate approved themes and editorial angles; track each account and publication link. TikTok and Instagram are the main options; Shorts or Facebook can be included in the agreed mix.
  6. Report and refine.Provide monthly account-level output and performance reporting: links, posting volume, views, engagement and available geography. Use the stronger formats to guide the next content plan.

Account management and service terms

Account availability

If a managed account becomes unavailable, we investigate the platform decision and report the interruption. Our proposed service commitment is a compliant replacement within seven days where permitted, with the affected account’s service fee paused until it resumes.

If replacement is not permitted or feasible, agree the credit or removal of that account from scope. Platform restrictions are not bypassed. Delivered and missed publications are recorded in the monthly report.

Ownership and access

Before setup, the order identifies who owns the accounts, who holds administrative access and what happens to the profiles and content when the service ends. Ownership transfer is not implied by a monthly operations fee.

Reklamatic manages the daily operation. You receive the agreed approval and reporting access, with public links to published posts.

Rights and corrections

You approve the source library, artist identity, music usage and destination links. We prepare content within those permissions and apply the agreed promotional disclosures.

On a rights complaint, we stop scheduling the affected material and request removal from managed accounts within 24 hours. Revision requests and removals are tracked with the relevant asset.

Working across several artists

Each artist can have a separate identity, content library and account group. For example, 10 accounts for each of three artists still totals 30 accounts and $5,400 + tax per month.

If Eluniboss manages the artist relationship, we can agree reporting under your identity. You remain the point of contact; account ownership, approvals and any client-protection provisions are written into the order.
